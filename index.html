Conditions Treated

Arizona Advanced Psychiatric Care provides evaluation and medication management for a range of psychiatric conditions in adults, including:

  • Depression
  • Anxiety
  • ADHD
  • Bipolar Disorder
  • PTSD
  • OCD

This list is not exhaustive. Additional conditions may be evaluated and treated during your appointment.

What to Expect

  1. Step 01

    Initial Evaluation

    Your first appointment is a 60-minute psychiatric evaluation. Carrie will listen to your concerns, review your health history and current medications, and discuss your goals for care.

  2. Step 02

    Treatment Plan

    You and Carrie will develop a personalized treatment plan based on your needs and preferences. This may include medication management and referrals for additional support.

  3. Step 03

    Ongoing Follow-Ups

    Follow-up appointments focus on your progress, questions, and how treatment is working. Carrie will review your response to medication and make adjustments as needed.

About Carrie Bann

Carrie Bann is a board-certified psychiatric-mental health nurse practitioner (PMHNP-BC) and family nurse practitioner (FNP-BC) providing psychiatric care for adults in Mesa and across Arizona. She graduated from Rocky Mountain University of Health Professions in 2021 with advanced training in psychiatric mental health care. Carrie evaluates and treats anxiety, depression, ADHD, and other mental health conditions through psychiatric evaluations and medication management. Her approach is compassionate, individualized, and collaborative. She takes time to understand each patient’s experiences, concerns, and goals, and works with them to develop a personalized treatment plan in an environment where they feel comfortable, respected, and heard.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I schedule a new patient appointment?

Select “Book with Carrie” to view available appointments and schedule online. If you plan to pay out of pocket or need help scheduling, call the office. Availability varies; please check for current openings.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Please have a list of your current medications, supplements, and allergies ready. Relevant medical records or previous psychiatric evaluations may also be helpful. Complete any requested intake forms before your 60-minute appointment.

How do I request a prescription refill?

Please schedule a 15-minute refill appointment at least 3 business days before you run out of medication. The self-pay rate is $100; insurance costs depend on your plan. Carrie will review your medication and determine whether a refill is appropriate.

What do I need for a telehealth appointment?

You’ll need a phone, tablet, or computer with a camera and microphone, a reliable internet connection, and a private, quiet space. Follow the joining instructions provided for your appointment and test your audio and video beforehand. Let the practice know where you’ll be physically located so it can confirm whether a telehealth visit is available there.

What is the cancellation policy?

Please provide at least 24 hours’ notice to cancel your appointment. A $75 fee applies to cancellations made less than 24 hours in advance and to missed appointments.

What is the policy on controlled substances?

Prescribing decisions are based on an individual evaluation, clinical appropriateness, and applicable requirements. An appointment does not guarantee a prescription for a particular medication. Carrie will explain any follow-up, monitoring, or other requirements that apply to your treatment plan.
