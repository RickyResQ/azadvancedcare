Notice of Privacy Practices

Arizona Advanced Psychiatric Care, LLC
Effective date: October 1, 2026

THIS NOTICE DESCRIBES HOW MEDICAL INFORMATION ABOUT YOU MAY BE USED AND DISCLOSED AND HOW YOU CAN GET ACCESS TO THIS INFORMATION. PLEASE REVIEW IT CAREFULLY.

This notice applies to Arizona Advanced Psychiatric Care, LLC and care provided by Carrie Bann through the practice. It describes protected health information we hold in connection with your care, including psychiatric evaluation, medication management and telehealth services.

Privacy contact: Carrie Bann
Telephone: (520) 527-4190
Mail: 6239 E. Brown Rd., Suite 118, Mesa, AZ 85205

Your rights and how to use them

Contact our privacy contact above to ask questions or exercise these rights. Call the number above for assistance. Send written requests to Carrie Bann at the office mailing address above, identifying the request and providing a way to contact you. We may verify your identity or authority before releasing information. Do not send clinical information to our general-business email.

See or obtain your records

You may request inspection or an electronic or paper copy of information in your designated record set, including medical and billing records. We will provide the requested form or format when readily producible, or agree on an alternative. We respond within the time required by applicable law. A reasonable, permitted cost-based copying fee may apply. Limited exceptions can apply, including to separately maintained psychotherapy notes. If access is denied, we will explain the reason in writing and any available review rights.

Ask for a correction

You may ask us in writing to amend information you believe is inaccurate or incomplete and explain why. We normally act within 60 days; if a permitted extension is needed, we will explain the reason and the new deadline. If we deny your request, we will give a written explanation and tell you how to submit a statement of disagreement.

Request private communications

You may ask us to contact you in a particular way or at a different address. We will accommodate reasonable requests. Tell our privacy contact how and where you want to be contacted.

Ask for limits on use or sharing

You may request restrictions on information used or shared for treatment, payment or health care operations, or with people involved in your care. We generally do not have to agree, but we must honor a qualifying request not to disclose information to your health plan for payment or operations when it concerns an item or service you paid for in full out of pocket and disclosure is not required by law. Tell us before the information is sent. An agreed restriction may have an emergency-treatment exception.

Your rights and permitted uses

Receive an accounting or a copy of this notice

You may request an accounting of certain disclosures made during the prior six years. The accounting excludes disclosures for treatment, payment and health care operations, disclosures you authorized, and other legally excluded disclosures. One accounting in a 12-month period is free. We will tell you in advance of a permitted fee for additional requests so you can change or withdraw the request. You may obtain a paper copy of this notice even if you agreed to receive it electronically.

Have a representative act for you

A person with legal authority, such as an authorized health care decision maker, may exercise rights on your behalf. We will verify that authority and apply any exceptions required or permitted by law.

Treatment payment and health care operations

Subject to the additional protections described below, we may use and disclose information for these purposes without a separate HIPAA authorization:

  • Treatment: coordinating your care, such as sharing relevant medication information with a clinician treating you or a pharmacy filling a prescription.
  • Payment: obtaining payment for services, such as sending diagnosis and service information to your health plan to process a claim.
  • Health care operations: managing and improving the practice, such as reviewing care quality, training staff, obtaining professional advice or conducting compliance activities.

Service providers performing functions involving protected information for us must meet applicable privacy requirements. We limit uses, disclosures and requests to the minimum necessary where the law requires it.

People involved in your care

We may share information directly relevant to the involvement of a family member, friend or another person you identify in your care or payment, with your agreement or opportunity to object. If you cannot make the decision, we may use professional judgment to share relevant information in your best interests, as the law permits. Similar rules can permit disaster-relief notifications. Additional state or federal restrictions still apply.

Other legally permitted or required disclosures

Subject to applicable legal conditions and the stronger protections described below, information may be used or disclosed:

  • To comply with law and to HHS for compliance oversight; for authorized public-health reporting, abuse or neglect reporting, health oversight, and preventing a serious and imminent threat.
  • For research when authorization or legally required protections, such as an approved waiver, apply; to authorized organ-procurement organizations; and to coroners, medical examiners or funeral directors for authorized duties.
  • For qualifying workers’ compensation, law-enforcement, military, national-security or other specialized government purposes; for permitted correctional-institution disclosures; and in judicial or administrative proceedings when required conditions are met. A request or subpoena alone does not remove applicable privacy protections.

Additional protections and our responsibilities

Substance use disorder and state protections

If we hold substance use disorder records protected by 42 CFR Part 2, those protections apply in addition to HIPAA. Records received from a Part 2 program, and testimony about their contents, will not be used or disclosed in civil, criminal, administrative or legislative proceedings against you without your written consent or a qualifying court order issued after notice and an opportunity to be heard. A court order must be accompanied by a subpoena or other legal requirement compelling disclosure. A general treatment, payment and operations consent does not authorize those proceedings.

Where permitted, Part 2 records received under a valid treatment, payment and health care operations consent may be used or redisclosed under HIPAA, subject to Part 2’s continuing limits. We will follow any more protective applicable state or federal requirements. Arizona law protects the confidentiality of medical and behavioral-health records and limits disclosures to legally authorized circumstances.

Uses requiring your written permission

We generally need your written authorization for uses or disclosures of separately maintained psychotherapy notes, for marketing that requires authorization, and for sale of protected health information. Limited legal exceptions may apply. Other uses or disclosures not described in this notice require your written authorization. You may revoke an authorization in writing, except to the extent we have already relied on it or another legal exception applies. Contact our privacy contact for instructions.

We do not use your health information for fundraising.

Our responsibilities and changes to this notice

We are legally required to protect the privacy of your protected health information, provide this notice of our duties and practices, and notify affected individuals after a breach of unsecured protected health information as required by law. We must follow the notice currently in effect. We reserve the right to revise this notice and apply permitted changes to all information we maintain, including previously collected information. A revised notice will be available on request, at our office and on our website.

Questions or complaints

Contact Carrie Bann using the contact details at the beginning of this notice. To file a complaint with the practice, call the number above or send a written complaint to Carrie Bann at the office mailing address. You may also complain to the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services Office for Civil Rights at https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/filing-a-complaint/index.html, by calling 1-877-696-6775, or by writing to 200 Independence Avenue, S.W., Washington, D.C. 20201. We will not retaliate against you for filing a complaint.
