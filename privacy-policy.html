About this policy

This policy explains how we handle information connected with azadvancedcare.com. Our separate Notice of Privacy Practices explains our responsibilities and your rights for protected health information associated with care.

Information connected with the website

Our website provides information about adult psychiatric evaluations and medication management, in-person care in Mesa, and telehealth in Arizona. It does not have an intake form or a place to upload medical records.

Cloudflare provides website hosting, delivery, security and analytics services. Cloudflare Web Analytics is enabled to measure website usage and performance. Technical information about visits and requests may be processed, including IP addresses, browser or device information, requested pages and network or performance information. Website infrastructure also has request logging and network-error reporting enabled. These services support delivery, reliability, security and understanding how the website is used.

Technical information may be handled by Cloudflare and made available to the practice through its service tools. Cloudflare’s privacy information is available at https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/.

Contacting the practice

If you contact us, we receive the contact information and message you choose to provide and use it to respond and handle your inquiry. Email and communications service providers process those communications. Our business email, carrie@azadvancedcare.com, forwards to a Yahoo mailbox and is for general business inquiries only. Do not send medical details, records, identification documents, insurance information or urgent clinical requests to that address. Call (520) 527-4190 for the appropriate way to contact the practice about care.

External scheduling and other links

Our scheduling link opens Headway, an external service. Information you provide there is handled under that service’s applicable privacy notices and terms. Review those notices before entering personal or health information. Clicking an external link may send technical information, such as your IP address and browser information, to that service; our scheduling link also includes marketing attribution parameters.

Your choices and privacy questions

You can use your browser’s privacy settings to control cookies and similar technologies. These settings do not necessarily stop all server-side processing. For website privacy questions, including questions about information handling or retention, contact Carrie Bann at (520) 527-4190 or the mailing address below. We will handle requests under applicable law. Patient-record requests should follow our Notice of Privacy Practices.

Changes and contact information

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our website or practices. The revised policy will display its effective date.
